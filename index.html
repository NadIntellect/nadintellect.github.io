Current Research

E3-A PRE-PILOT

E3-A is a calibration and instrumentation experiment designed to test whether a constrained AI-output pipeline can produce a reproducible, decoder-achievable lower bound on information transmitted by a model while separately detecting incidental leakage through the release decision.

Engineering readiness is not scientific validation.

The QA results below confirm that the implementation is ready to run. They are not evidence for or against the scientific hypothesis. That evidence can only come from the live pilot.

The Next Intelligence Changes the Question

Artificial intelligence is becoming increasingly capable. The long-term challenge is not only how capable future systems become, but whether increasingly powerful intelligence can remain understandable, testable and controllable.

NadIntellect Lab is an independent AI research laboratory developing and experimentally testing approaches to this unresolved problem.

We do not assume the answer.We build experiments that can prove us wrong.

Research Philosophy

  • Falsifiable by design
  • Evidence before confidence
  • Staged validation
  • Failure is information

Each stage is designed to produce evidence before the program advances to the next one. A negative or uninterpretable result is not hidden; it changes the research path.

Stage 05 / Long-term research object

AI Cognitive Reactor

From digital experiments to physical validation.

The AI Cognitive Reactor is a physical, instrumented experimental environment for testing system-level properties of architectures for controllable superintelligence under reproducible and adversarial conditions.

A research object — not a claim of completed validation.
