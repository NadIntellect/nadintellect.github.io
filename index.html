Mission

We are looking beyond the horizon of today’s LLM capabilities — toward systems whose intelligence may significantly exceed human intelligence.

Our goal is to move from theoretical models and digital experiments to a physical experimental reactor for controlled work with superintelligence.

The technical architecture of NadIntellect is not currently public. The project is working on intellectual-property protection and patent strategy.

Research trajectory

Where we are going

We are building a program of sequential experiments in which each next stage builds on the results of the previous one.

  1. 01

    Theoretical construction

    Formalization of key principles, identification of weak points, and technical testing of assumptions.

  2. 02

    Digital experiments

    Testing individual mechanisms and measurement methods under controlled digital conditions.

  3. 03

    Live testing

    Experiments with real, powerful language models.

  4. 04

    Scaling

    More complex conditions, stronger models, and more demanding tests.

  5. 05

    Physical reactor

    Transition to a physical experimental system for controlled work with superintelligence.
